# Insight copy

Use neutral language that describes the data without grading the person.

- Ahead: `You are 2 completions ahead of last week.`
- On pace: `You are on pace for your weekly goal.`
- Behind: `Two more completions would put you back on pace.`
- No history: `Complete a habit to start your first trend.`

Avoid shame-oriented words such as `failed`, `lazy`, or `missed`.
